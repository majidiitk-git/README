# Hi, I'm Abdul Majid!

**PhD in Chemical Engineering | Computational & Systems Biology | Mathematical Modeling | AI/ML Disease Research**

I am a computational researcher working at the intersection of **mathematical modeling, computational system biology and machine learning** to understand complex biological and disease systems.

I completed my **PhD in Chemical Engineering at IIT Kanpur**, where I developed nonlinear and kinetic ODE-based models to study **amyloid-beta aggregation dynamics and therapeutic inhibition mechanisms in Alzheimer's disease**.

Currently, I am working as a **Postdoctoral Research Associate at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc)**, where my research focuses on mathematical and mechanistic learning approaches for understanding **cancer progression and therapy response**.

## Research Journey

### Alzheimer's Disease

During my doctoral and postdoctoral research at **IIT Kanpur**, I developed mathematical and kinetic models to investigate:

* Amyloid-beta nucleated polymerization
* Reactive oxygen species (ROS)-mediated amyloid aggregation
* Inhibitory effects of small-molecule compounds
* Inhibition and degradation of amyloid-beta fibrils
* Reaction kinetics and polydispersity of amyloid aggregation
* AI/ML-assisted biomarker discovery and drug repurposing

My work combines **ODE modeling, parameter estimation, numerical simulation, optimization, sensitivity analysis, and experimental-data fitting**.

###  Cancer Modeling

At **IISc Bangalore**, I am currently working on mathematical and ML-based approaches to understand:

* Cancer progression
* Tumor growth dynamics
* Therapy response
* Dynamical behavior of cancer cell populations
* Mechanistic and data-driven modeling of cancer systems
